5. Technical Defense & Viva Q&A

Exam Defense & Architectural Trade-offs

Key questions the examiners will ask and our validated technical defenses.

Q: Why tvOS instead of an iPad stand on the counter?

Counter space is messy, wet, and hazardous. Tablets get splashed with oil, flour, and sauce. The living room or kitchen TV offers an unencumbered 10-foot communal screen visible to everyone in the room without risking delicate electronics.

Q: How do you handle network drops during live cooking?

Apple Multipeer Connectivity establishes an ad-hoc Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth Low Energy mesh between devices. It does not require active internet connection after recipe assets are cached, guaranteeing zero dropout during high-heat pan searing.
